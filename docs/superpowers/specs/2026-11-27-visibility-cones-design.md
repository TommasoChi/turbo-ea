# Visibility cones

Owner decisions recorded. Baseline: `main` at `1ae9ae9e5`, including Cost Controller (PR #94). A visibility cone is the set of card grants a manager receives from stakeholder assignments held by people in subordinate Organization positions.

## Why

A manager needs to work on the same cards as the people they manage. Their own manager needs the same reach through every level beneath them. Today these grants have to be assigned card by card. A manager can miss a card even when their reporting line is already recorded in Organization.

## Goals

- Derive each manager's card grants from the Organization Parent tree and current stakeholder assignments.
- Apply the grants to reads and the writes those stakeholder roles allow. Keep existing application and function gates.
- Explain inherited grants in the card UI. Keep personal views and actions tied to direct assignments.
- Ship three independently verifiable changes. Enable the cone only with consistent card and list authorization.

## Non-goals

- Inheriting another person's application role, application permission keys, type or subtype cells, or access to an application function.
- Reporting lines between other card types, temporary or per-user delegation, or new tables for people, positions or grants.
- Fixing the broader hidden-card write response, bulk-edit authorization or who can assign data-bearing roles on ordinary cards. Those concerns were raised in the brief and need their own changes. Position grants and Organization reparenting are in scope.

## The rule

A position is an Organization card. A position holder has a direct assignment to a role on that card whose definition is marked as a position role. The seeded Responsible is the default position role. Admin explicitly assigns it when creating an Organization. Without an assignment, the position is vacant. Observer cannot confer a position.

When Admin has enabled the workspace switch, start at each valid position that user U holds. Follow `parent_id` down through active Organization positions at every depth, with cycle protection. Collect the distinct holders of all strict descendants. A vacant active position contributes no holder and preserves the path to its children. Multiple holders at a position all count. A person with several positions uses the union of their subtrees. A person reached more than once contributes each direct assignment once. Exclude U from the subordinate set. Peers and ancestors contribute nothing through those relationships.

Archiving or deleting a position interrupts inheritance for its orphaned descendants. An orphaned branch cannot supply grants to its former managers or start a cone of its own. Admin restores normal evaluation by explicitly reattaching the branch to a valid active branch. Restoring the old parent or using a lifecycle child strategy must not silently resume inheritance. Direct grants survive the interruption.

For a target card C of any type, the effective stakeholder grants of U come from U's direct roles on C and the direct roles on C of the subordinates just found. The rule also applies when C is an Organization card. Resolve each role by C's type and use its current active definition. A role that is undefined or archived grants nothing. Direct assignments retain their current authority subject to the Organization write rule. No inherited assignment row is created.

The effective permission is still the union of the caller's own application grant and qualifying card grants. The caller must satisfy independent application function requirements. A subordinate's powerful application role contributes zero grants. Inherited `card.view_costs` and `card.edit_costs` are card grants subject to the shipped cost rules. The card and its costs must be visible before a cost edit. A Cost Controller assignment can give a manager cost access on the target card while leaving general editing unavailable. PPM still requires the manager's own function permissions. Licensing keeps its existing conditions with the inherited card grants as inputs. Inherit all permissions from qualifying stakeholder roles, including `card.manage_stakeholders`. Inherited `card.view` can grant access despite a type or subtype `View: Deny`, just as a direct stakeholder grant can. To write through an inherited grant, the manager must also be able to read the target card. The read and write grants can come from different sources. Organization is read-only for non-Admin users, regardless of direct or inherited grants. Only Admin can change the chart.

Example: the director holds IT Direction. The manager holds its child IT Governance. You and your colleague hold separate children of IT Governance. Assume each person has a direct read-and-edit stakeholder role on their own example card, no other relevant grant, and no applicable deny. The example cards are Applications. “Read and edit” means the card's general data.

| Reader | Your card | Colleague's card | Manager's card | Director's card |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| You | Read and edit | — | — | — |
| Colleague | — | Read and edit | — | — |
| Manager | Read and edit | Read and edit | Read and edit | — |
| Director | Read and edit | Read and edit | Read and edit | Read and edit |

## Acceptance

- The example yields the four stated rows of read and edit rights. A subordinate with a powerful application role contributes zero privileges without relevant stakeholder assignments.
- The cone works for direct reports, deeper descendants, multiple positions and multiple holders. Peers and ancestors never contribute through those relationships. Changing the chart or removing a grant takes effect after commit.
- Users without direct or derived access receive the same hidden-card behavior as today. No hidden card appears in a list, count, reference, nested payload or source explanation. The extended parity tests and sentinel sweep prove denial and positive access.
- A manager with an inherited Cost Controller role can manage costs without general card edit when the card, its costs and the relevant function are available. General card editing and cost visibility still require a separate cost-edit grant to write costs. License permissions and personal views keep their existing rules. On Organization, only Admin can use `me/observe`. On other types it keeps its existing behavior. The API verifies inherited reads despite View deny, the read prerequisite for inherited writes, inherited stakeholder management, and Admin-only Organization writes.
- A vacant position preserves traversal. An archived or deleted position interrupts the orphaned branch in both directions until reattachment. The audit identifies the person who acted.
- The workspace switch defaults off for new and existing workspaces. Import leaves the destination switch as it was. Old bundles give no positive position-role flag. The UI matches server authorization and the unrestricted fast path stays intact.
